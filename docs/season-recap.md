# Season recap

Part of Frescati's AGENTS.md context. See the root `AGENTS.md` for the app overview, `docs/ratings.md` for the ledger every number here comes from, and `docs/notifications.md` for `recapReady`.

- **A season is over when there is nothing left in it to happen.** Every game that isn't cancelled has a ledger entry. There is no clock in it: the confirm button has no time on it, so an admin can rate the last game before its slot ends, and that confirmation is the one chance the backend gets to notice. `isSeasonFinished` in `shared/recap.ts` is the rule, and both the backend and the backfill call it. A game that was played and never scored holds the recap back on purpose: a recap with a result missing would disagree with the table beside it, and the fix is one score or one cancellation away. A season with nothing left that counts was never played, so it never finishes.
- **The backend decides when, and the client only reads the stamp.** `announceRecapIfReady` runs after `finaliseGame` confirms a game and when `onGameWrite` sees a game cancelled, which are the only two ways the last missing piece can arrive. It stamps `seasons/{id}.recapReadyAt` in a transaction, so of two confirmations racing at the end of a season exactly one writes it, and only that one sends the notification. A rating replay rewrites ledger entries and never re-announces. Clients cannot write the field; `firestore.rules` freezes it beside `confirmedAt` in one `diff()`, because the season update rule sits close to Firestore's 1000-expression ceiling and a second one tipped it over.
- **It opens on the last result, not on the last vote.** The final game's man-of-the-match vote opens at the same moment the recap does, so the MOTM award, and the rating bonus its replay adds, can still move for a day or two. Nothing on a slide is stored: `getClubRecap`, `getAwards`, `getBanter` and `getPersonalRecap` run when the slides open, so whoever watches later sees the settled numbers.
- **The takeover.** `useSeasonRecapTakeover` on the season home sends somebody to `/s/{id}/recap` when the season is stamped, they have an appearance in its ledger, and the season is not in their `users/{uid}.recapsWatched`. Same arrival rules as the live-game redirect, shared in `hooks/useArrival.ts`: on the load and on a return to the foreground, once per season per session. It pushes rather than replaces, unlike the live-game redirect: the chevron goes back when it can, and a replace would make that step skip the season home. The recap screen claims the session mark itself, so a recap opened from the notification or a profile does not bounce off the home screen when it is closed. Reaching the last slide writes `recapsWatched` with `arrayUnion`, and only for your own season and never for a preview.
- **The slides.** The club in numbers, the podium (ties share a step), games to remember, then the awards: man of the match, iron man with a perfect-attendance line, biggest climber, longest winning run. Then the banter: most no-shows, Last-minute Lucy and the wooden spoon. Then your own season, ending on a goodbye. Every award names everybody level at the top, and an award nobody won is left out rather than drawn empty. Money never appears.
- **Why the banter is there, and where it stops.** The group asked for it. Each one has a floor so it lands on somebody who earned it. The wooden spoon needs three games, `SPOON_MIN_APPEARANCES`, so a player who came twice and lost twice is unlucky, not bottom. No-shows only count the `absent` mark an admin set. Last-minute Lucy counts answers whose `updatedAt` falls in the 24 hours before kickoff; responses keep no history, so it counts answers last touched late, not how often somebody changed their mind.
- **Your people.** The teammate you won most with and your nemesis, from `getPlayerLinks` over this season's games only. A nemesis needs two meetings, `NEMESIS_MIN_GAMES`, and a losing record against them. Ledger entries written before teams were stored are skipped, the same as everywhere else, so an old season may have no people slide.
- **Somebody else's season.** A profile lists each stamped season the player appeared in, linking to `/s/{id}/recap?player={uid}`. The personal slides are theirs, and watching it does not count as yours.
- **The debug preview.** `/debug` has a Preview recap button for the season picked above, opening `?preview=1`. It works only for an app admin, on any season, from the games rated so far, with a "Preview, N of M games" badge on the first slide. It never writes `recapsWatched`, so it doesn't use up the real one.
- **Seasons that ended before this shipped.** `pnpm --filter backend backfill-recap-ready` stamps them and marks them watched for everybody who played, so they get a replay card and profile links without taking over anybody's home or sending anything. Deploy the functions first, then run it once; `--dry-run` says what it would write.
- **Testing.** `shared/recap.test.ts` covers every number. `backend/tests/recap.test.ts` covers the stamp and the single send, and the backfill is in `backfills.test.ts`. The takeover is `hooks/useRecapTakeover.test.tsx`, and the slides are `components/recap/*.test.tsx`. `e2e/recap.spec.ts` plays the seeded `Winter league 2025`, which is over, rated and stamped, as a different player per viewport, because watching it writes to the profile and both viewports share one database.
