# Matchday

Part of Frescati's AGENTS.md context. See the root `AGENTS.md` for the app overview, `docs/ratings.md` for the ledger every number here comes from, and `docs/season-recap.md` for the takeover this one gives way to.

Two screens around the football. The **programme**, before it: tonight's odds, everybody's form, the rivalry and a few storylines. The **full-time card**, after it: one player's own night, which takes over the season home the first time they come back and can be shared as an image. The spec is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-matchday-design.md`.

- **Nothing is stored.** `shared/matchday.ts` and `shared/fullTime.ts` work everything out from the ledger, the lineup and the result document, every time a screen opens. A corrected score or a rating replay rewrites those, so both screens tell the corrected story without anything to keep in step. A Cloud Function writing a preview and a card per player was considered and dropped for that reason. Every replay would have had to rewrite them too.
- **The programme is found, not announced.** On game day, the kickoff's date in the season's own timezone from midnight until the final whistle (`isGameDay`), the season home's next-game card grows a strip and the game screen a card, both into `/s/{id}/g/{gameId}/programme`. There is no push. The reminder already reaches the people who haven't answered, at `reminderHours`, which defaults to 72 and 24, so it does not land on game day and never reaches anybody who said In. A noon push of its own was offered and turned down. The route itself works any day, so a link passed around still opens, and only the ways in are kept to game day.
- **The odds are the chance of topping the table, and they only move when the teams do.** Team strength is the squad's average Elo from the lineup document, which already holds a seed for anybody unrated. Two teams play one match, so it is the Elo win probability. Three or four play a round robin, so `getTeamOdds` plays the fixture list out 2000 times with a PRNG seeded from the game and the lineup, and a shared top spot splits that evening. Whole percentages, largest remainder, always 100. No odds before there are teams.
- **Every storyline has a floor**, so nothing reads "won 1 in a row". A rivalry needs three meetings on opposite teams (`RIVALRY_MIN_MEETINGS`), a run three games (`RUN_MIN`), a return 28 days away (`RETURN_MIN_DAYS`), a man of the match run two votes (`MOTM_RUN_MIN`), and a milestone is one display point below a multiple of ten. At most one per player, at most three in all, winning runs first. A losing run is something to end, never something to laugh at. The banter belongs to the recap.
- **Form reads ledger positions.** W is an outright first, D a first shared with another team, L anything else. An old entry with no `teams` map cannot tell a shared first from an outright one, so it reads W, which is how the season table counts it. The rivalry skips those entries altogether, because without the map a teammate and an opponent look the same.
- **The card agrees with the team sheet.** The rating comes off the result's `RatingDelta` and goes through `RatingMovement` and `movementLabel`, so a `+2` here is a `+2` there. The finish and the record come off the result's standings. Up to two highlights: a winning run started, extended or ended, a climb of two places or into first on `getSeasonTable`, a career best once past `PROVISIONAL_GAMES`, a first win. No man of the match, because the vote opens at the moment the card does.
- **The takeover gives way.** `useHomeTakeovers` runs the three in order: the live-game redirect, then the recap, then the card. The card skips a season with `recapReadyAt`, since the recap is the send-off, and skips arriving during a game the viewer is In for. It only ever shows the season's latest confirmed game, and only within seven days of `finalisedAt`, so somebody back after a month is not greeted by last month.
- **Seen is per device, and it is a time.** `lib/fullTimeSeen.ts` keeps the latest `finalisedAt` this device has closed or shared in `localStorage`. No rules change and nothing on the profile, at the price of the card appearing once on each device. A time rather than a game id so a replay that confirms a game again brings the corrected card back. Like the recap, the card pushes rather than replaces, and claims a session mark so Back lands on a home that stays put. Storage that throws counts as not recorded, so the jump is not taken.
- **Sharing draws on a canvas.** `lib/fullTimeImage.ts` paints the card at 1080 by 1350 and hands the PNG to `navigator.share`, or downloads it where a phone cannot share files. The colours are the StyleX tokens, read off hidden swatches inside the card with `getComputedStyle`, because a canvas cannot use a CSS variable and a hex value written down twice drifts. A DOM-to-image library lost on web fonts and the CSP. A `next/og` route lost because the server would have to read Firestore.
- **Testing.** `shared/matchday.test.ts` and `shared/fullTime.test.ts` cover every number and floor. The screens, the strip, the takeover and the image are in `frontend/components/programme`, `components/fullTime`, `hooks/useFullTimeTakeover.test.tsx` and `lib/fullTimeImage.test.ts`. `e2e/matchday.spec.ts` opens the programme by its address, since the seeded next game is days away, and lets the card take over Sunday Kickabout, whose last game every member played. Every other spec refuses the card's session key, `frescati:full-time`, the same way it refuses the live-game redirect's.
