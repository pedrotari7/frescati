# Finances

Part of Frescati's CLAUDE.md context. See the root `CLAUDE.md` for the app overview, `docs/members-vs-extras.md` for what a role means and `docs/no-show.md` for the `absent` flag this reads.

- **A season costs money, and two collections carry the whole answer.** `seasons/{id}/dues/{dueId}` is one document per charge, `seasons/{id}/expenses/{expenseId}` is one per thing the group bought, and `/s/[seasonId]/finances` behind the Squad tab reads both. Two kinds of charge and nothing else: a member owes their share of the season's bill, an extra owes a fee for each game they actually played.
- **The fees live on the season document, beside `balance`,** because they are settings an admin types on the same form and an allowlisted map is the pattern that document already uses. `total` is the season's invoice, `perGame` is what an extra pays, `swish` is the number that collects. `feesFor` fills in `DEFAULT_FEES`, so a season created before any of this existed reads as free rather than as broken.
- **The bill is split equally and rounded up.** `entryShare(total, memberCount)`, `Math.ceil`, and the direction is the whole point: 31240 across 18 members is 1735.55 each, so rounding down leaves the group ten kronor short of a pitch it has already booked. Over by eight kronor is a rounding error, short is a phone call. An empty squad is zero rather than a division by zero, since a season nobody has joined has nobody to charge, which is not the same as a bill of nothing.
- **A charge is stored, not derived, and `shared/kit.ts` argues the opposite case for kit and is right about that one.** The difference is that a charge gets *settled*. "Paid" is a stored fact and it is stored against something, so if the charge underneath it is a calculation then an admin marking an extra absent a week later silently deletes a charge somebody has already paid and leaves the payment pointing at nothing, which is exactly the side effect `docs/no-show.md` rules out when it says the flag moves nothing else. Storing it freezes the amount and the role at the moment it was raised, which disposes of the two awkward cases for free: moving the fee in March does not re-price February, and an extra later added to `memberUids` still owes for the games they played as an extra.
- **So the app does the arithmetic and an admin does the writing.** `planDues` works out every charge that ought to exist, `missingDues` subtracts the ones that do, and the screen offers to raise the difference. It is deliberately two presses, one to say how many are missing and one to raise them, because "raise 43 charges" is not a thing to find out you have done. `missingDues` compares **by id and nothing else**, so a charge that exists is left alone whatever it now says, an admin may have waived it, edited it or marked it paid, and none of those are things a sweep should undo.
- **The document id is derived from what the charge is for**, `entry_{uid}` or `game_{gameId}_{uid}`, and that is what makes the button safe to press twice. A random id would let a second press, or two admins pressing at once, charge the whole squad again. Belt and braces in the rules as well: `onlySettles()` allows an update to touch only `status`, `settledAt`, `settledBy`, `note` and `amount`, so a second sweep writing the same id with a fresh `createdAt` is refused rather than quietly resetting a paid charge back to owing.
- **The sweep is the only one-shot `getDocs` read in the app,** roughly one query per played game, which is why it sits behind an explicit admin tap instead of a subscription on page load. A few hundred reads once in a while, in exchange for no Cloud Function, no new function-owned field on a hot document and no backfill for existing seasons. `fetchPlayedGameResponses` is the read; `owesForGame` is the test, `role === 'extra'` and confirmed and not absent, both predicates taken from `shared/game.ts` rather than re-tested here.
- **Only one of the two sides is a pot.** The entry fees exist to pay one invoice, so the question about them is never "how much is there to spend", it is "is the season paid for": `EntryFund` has a `target` and a `short`, floored at zero because a season cannot be more than paid for and rounding the shares up means a full squad usually pays a few kronor over. The extras' money is the money there is to spend, and an expense carries **no field for which pot it came out of**, because a field with one legal value is a field that will eventually hold the other one.
- **The books are the one thing in this app that every signed-in user cannot read.** `isSeasonSquad` in the rules is the union of `memberUids` and `adminUids` off a single `seasonData()`. The README's case for an open group is that an extra has to be able to find a game and put their hand up, and none of that reasoning reaches "who still hasn't paid". An extra falls to the second clause, `resource.data.uid == request.auth.uid`, which standing alone denies a list exactly as it does on `watchers`, so the app asks with `where('uid', '==', them)` and gets only their own. Expenses are readable by anyone signed in, since an expense names a ball rather than a person.
- **`fees` had to go into `seasonShapeOk()`, and that is the change here that could have locked every admin out of every season.** That allowlist is a `keys().hasOnly([...])` against `request.resource.data`, which on an update is the *merged* document, so the moment one season carried the field every unrelated season edit would have been denied. `feesShapeOk(request.resource.data.get('fees', {}))` joins `balanceShapeOk` at the end of it, and `rules/firestore.test.ts` pins a plain season update with `fees` present for exactly this reason.
- **Both Swish mechanisms are strings, and neither one talks to Swish.** The QR payload is specified, `C<payee>;<amount>;<message>;0`, so it is the path that is always drawn. The `C` prefix reads like "company" and works with an ordinary mobile number, which is the only reason a football group can use any of this. The `swish://payment?data=` URL is reverse-engineered, so it is an extra button under a `pointer: coarse` media query and never the only way to pay. The lock mask is `0`, everything locked, because the message is how an admin works out whose payment landed and an editable one is a payment nobody can match. Nothing calls `mpc.getswish.net`: it is not in the CSP `connect-src`, and a payment screen that waits on a third party is a payment screen that fails at the pitch.
- **Marking a payment is a claim about money, so it is signed.** `settledBy == request.auth.uid` in the rules, and `settlementIsCoherent()` enforces the union in `shared/types.ts`: a charge is either owing and unsigned, or settled and carrying who settled it and when. Without it the document admits a payment nobody recorded taking, and an owing charge still holding the receipt from the time it was paid. Paying through the app ticks nothing off by itself, which the screen says out loud twice.
- **No backend.** `onSeasonDeleted`'s `recursiveDelete` already takes both subcollections, and nothing here needs privilege. The one privileged thing finances could want is a collected total an extra could see, which needs a function-owned summary document maintained by a trigger, and that is a whole Cloud Function so a guest can see one number. Left out on purpose, one trigger away if it matters. Both collections are in `forgetPlayer.ts`.
