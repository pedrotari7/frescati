# Season sign-up

Part of Frescati's AGENTS.md context. See the root `AGENTS.md` for the app overview, `docs/finances.md` for the entry fee this raises, and `docs/notifications.md` for the three kinds it sends.

- **A new season does not start with a squad, it starts with a question.** Every season is created `draft`, labelled "Signing up", with `memberUids: []`, and everybody in the app is asked whether they are joining. An admin confirms when enough have said yes, and the yeses become the squad. Nobody is added by hand, and nobody is charged who did not say yes.
- **An answer is a document, and a missing one is the third state.** `seasons/{id}/signups/{uid}` holds `status: 'yes' | 'no'`, written by the player. No document means they have not answered, the same as a game response, so nothing ever writes a placeholder. A "no" is stored because an admin has to tell somebody who declined from somebody who has not looked yet. People can change their answer, or take it back, right up to confirm.
- **`answeredBy` is there so a wrong answer has an author.** A season admin can answer for anybody and the rules make them set `answeredBy` to their own uid. A player's own answer must not carry one. It is the reason a scoreline is signed, and the player is not told, for the reasons in `docs/answering-for-somebody.md`.
- **`signupClosesOn` stops the reminders and nothing else.** An answer that arrives after it is still worth having until somebody confirms, so the rules do not look at the date. Confirming is what shuts the door, by moving the status on.
- **Status moves one way, and only the function moves it.** The value is `draft` and the label is "Signing up", so no stored data had to change when the label did. Only `confirmSeason` takes a season out of draft, and nothing puts one back. The season rules refuse any client update that crosses the line in either direction, which is why the status dropdown on the admin page is hidden for a draft and no longer offers Draft anywhere. A running season has games, answers and dues hanging off it that a sign-up knows nothing about.
- **A draft's squad is whatever the answers say, so a client cannot edit `memberUids` on one.** An admin with the squad screen open would be building a second roster around the first, and confirming would overwrite it. `copiedFrom` is frozen after creation for a related reason, below.
- **Games exist during sign-up and cannot be answered.** An admin can generate the calendar while the season is a draft, and players can read it. Every response write is refused by the rules, the player's own and the admin's proxy path, because with no squad the counters would file everybody as an extra. The screen shows the games with no In and Out buttons.
- **Where the question shows.** A card on every season home while any season is a draft the viewer has not answered, one per draft, with the dates, the venue and "About 1 736 kr each so far", which is `fees.total` over the yeses to date and moves as people answer. After an answer it folds to one line with Change. The draft's own page asks the same question and lists who has said yes, and the seasons list shows it with its pill. The card goes when the season is confirmed.

## Starting a season

- **A new season can be copied from an old one.** `seasons/new` opens with a Copy from picker, newest first, set to the newest season you may copy. The form fills in the venue, slot, minimum and the length of the season, and leaves the name blank. `nextSeasonDates` starts it on the first slot weekday strictly after the old end date, so a season that ended on its own weekday starts a week later, never the same day, and keeps the old season's length.
- **The rest is copied unseen and named in one summary line.** Deadline, reminder hours, balance, fees (the Swish number included) and the other admins come along through `copySeasonSettings`, and the line says what. They are edited afterwards on the admin settings page, which works on a draft like any other season. Building every setting into the create form would duplicate that page. A `balance` or `fees` the source never had is left out rather than written as `undefined`, which Firestore rejects.
- **A season admin can start the season that follows one they run, and only that.** An app admin can start any season, blank or copied. A season admin's create passes the rules only if `copiedFrom` is a season they run, so they cannot start a blank one. That makes `copiedFrom` the permission as well as the provenance, which is why the rules freeze it after creation: an admin who could repoint it later could pull in the kit of a season they do not run. The creator must also be in `adminUids`, so a season is never created orphaned. "Start next season" on a season's admin page opens the form with the picker already set.
- **The creator is an admin and not a member.** The create page used to write them into the squad, and that stops. For the length of the sign-up this breaks the rule the rest of the app keeps, that every admin is a member, and confirming puts it back.

## Quiet

- **A quiet season sends none of `signupOpen`, `signupReminder` and `seasonConfirmed`, and nothing else is muted.** It exists so the whole flow can be tried on the live app without messaging thirty people. The check sits in the callers: `opensSignup` in `onSeasonWrite`, `signupReminderDue` in the reminder sweep, and `confirmSeason` before it tells the squad. Answers, confirm, dues and kit behave exactly as they would in a loud season, and once the season is active its game notifications go out as usual whether or not it was quiet. Archive or delete a test season after confirming it.
- **Turning quiet off on a draft sends `signupOpen` then.** `onSeasonWrite` treats a draft going from quiet to loud as the opening of the sign-up, so a season set up and checked in private can go live without being recreated. The same function fires it for a draft created loud. Saving a draft that was already asking announces nothing.
- **The switch is on the create form and on the admin settings page while the season is a draft.** After confirm there is nothing left for it to mute.

## Reminders

- **`sendSignupReminders` runs every day at 18:00 Stockholm time and `signupReminderDue` decides whether today counts.** The nudge goes out every third civil day after the day the season was created, in the season's own timezone, until the close date, inclusive. A day that is not due costs one query for the drafts. It never fires for a quiet season, a season that is no longer a draft, or on the day it was created, since `signupOpen` has just asked.
- **It goes only to people with no answer.** That is the absence of a `signups` document, so somebody who said no is not chased. The preference is `reminders`, the same as `signupOpen`, because a person who muted being chased has said what they want. A season failing is reported and the sweep carries on, since the next due day is three days away.

## Confirming

- **Confirm is a callable function, because it has to land whole.** `confirmSeason` writes the squad, the admins, the status, the dues and the kit in one transaction. A confirm that half happened, members saved with the status still `draft`, or charges raised for a squad that was never written, is the failure it exists to rule out. The rules cannot express any of that, and they refuse every client write that would try. The answers are read inside the transaction, so a player changing their mind while it runs either makes it into the squad or makes the transaction retry against the new answer, and exactly one of two presses gets through. The loser finds the season active and is told so with `failed-precondition`.
- **The confirming admin has to have said yes.** They end up a member, which is what keeps their admin rights and lets them hold the kit nobody else can. The button is disabled with a line saying why until they have answered, and the function refuses without it.
- **Admins who did not say yes lose admin rights on this season.** `adminUids` becomes the admins who are also in the squad, which restores the rule that every admin is a member. This is the surprise in the flow, so the confirm sheet names each person it is about to demote.
- **Everybody who said yes becomes a member, and nobody else does.** `planConfirmation` in `shared/` decides who is in, who stays an admin, what each owes and where the kit goes, and the function only runs it inside a transaction, the same split as `owesForGame`. People who never answered stay unanswered and can still join later through `LateJoinSheet`, which prices a late fee. There is no cap, no minimum and no automatic confirmation. A season with one yes, the confirming admin's, is a valid confirm, and the sheet says the number before it is pressed.
- **Entry dues are raised at confirm, with the same ids the sweep uses.** One `entry_{uid}` per member at `entryShare(fees.total, members)`, so the books screen's sweep finds them and leaves them alone. With no fee set, nothing is raised.
- **The debt lock applies.** Each charge is owing the moment it is written, `onDueWrite` marks the member a debtor, and a member cannot answer In until they have paid. Paying is what makes the place real. Season admins are exempt, here as everywhere. `docs/finances.md` has the lock.
- **Kit is copied at confirm, never at creation.** A `KitItem.holderUid` must be in `memberUids`, and a draft has no members. At confirm each item of the `copiedFrom` season's kit is copied: whoever held it keeps it if they joined, and everything else goes to the confirming admin, who is a member by then.
- **`seasonConfirmed` replaces `dueRaised` for these charges, so nobody gets two messages.** It is the welcome and the bill in one: the title carries the amount, the body says where to Swish it and that the first In opens once it is paid. With no fee it says they are in and mentions no money. It has no preference switch, because the person asked to join and this is the answer. It is sent after the commit, one per member, reported and swallowed one by one. The squad is saved, and a push that failed is not a reason to take that back.
- **Nothing reopens it.** There is no way back to sign-up and no undoing a confirm. People who joined that should not have are removed from the squad screen like any other member.
