# Answering for somebody

Part of Frescati's AGENTS.md context. See the root `AGENTS.md` for the app overview, `docs/no-show.md` for the mark this hands over to at kick-off, and `docs/members-vs-extras.md` for what an extra's In does before an admin confirms it.

- **A season admin can write any answer on the roster, from the game screen.** Somebody texts the group instead of opening the app, and until this existed the organiser was booking a pitch against a headcount they knew was wrong. The security rules have always allowed it, `allow create, update: if isSeasonAdmin(seasonId)` on `seasons/{id}/games/{id}/responses/{uid}`, bounded to the shape a response has; what was missing was anywhere in the app to do it from.
- **Up to kick-off, and not a minute past it.** `canAnswerFor` in `shared/game.ts` is `open` or `locked`, which is the exact complement of `canReportAbsence` on every game where either question arises, and a unit test holds the two apart. Before kick-off an answer is a plan, and a plan somebody texted about is the organiser's to correct. After it the answer is history, and the way to record that somebody did not turn up is the mark beside their In rather than a rewrite of what they said. Offering both at once would be two ways to record the same evening, and only one of them keeps the distinction anybody asking wants. A cancelled game is neither: there is nothing to answer about a game that is off.
- **The rules are deliberately wider than the app.** They take an admin write on any game at any point in its life, because a roster that needs tidying after the fact is still theirs to tidy, and `setAbsent` and `setConfirmOverride` both go through that same rule past kick-off. `canAnswerFor` is the app deciding what to offer, not the boundary.
- **A pencil on the row, a sheet, then the dialog.** The roster groups already end in controls of their own, the spot for an extra and the nudge for somebody who has said nothing, and a second labelled button beside either takes the width the name needs. So the control is an icon, and its accessible name carries the person. It opens `AnswerSheet` rather than writing, because the row cannot know which of the three answers the admin came to give, and the sheet comes down before the dialog goes up so the two are never stacked on a phone. The one group with no pencil is Didn't show, and the omission is the point: rewriting what a no-show said is what that mark exists not to do.
- **The dialog says the part nobody would guess.** `notifyWatchers` leaves the player out of the push about their own answer, because normally they are the one who just gave it. Answering on their behalf is the case where that silence is wrong, and nothing else in the app fills it, so each of the three prompts ends by saying they are not told.
- **`role` is resolved from the season at write time, not carried over.** `getRole` reads `memberUids`, the same list the tally and the team sheet read, so an answer recorded for somebody who has since joined the squad is recorded as the member they now are. The admin rule pointedly does not check it, `roleMatchesMembership` asks about the writer and the writer is not the player, so nothing else would. The response they already hold goes into `setResponse` so their `respondedAt`, an admin's `confirmOverride` and an admin's `absent` all carry through: a confirmed extra moved to Out and back keeps their spot, which is the same promise the player's own buttons make.
- **Taking an answer away is a delete.** `null` from the sheet, `clearResponse` underneath, back to no document at all, because that absence is the real third state and never a stored value. It puts a member back on the list the reminders chase, which is the one thing the prompt for it says beyond the silence.
